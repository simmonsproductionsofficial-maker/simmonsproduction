About me

Hi, I'm Jaxson.

My name is Jaxson Simmons, and I'm a young photographer with a passion for capturing the moments that matter.

I'm 13 years old and just getting started in the world of sports photography, with a special interest in wrestling. Being a wrestler myself, I understand the hard work, dedication, and emotion that go into every match.

I started Simmons Productions to turn my passion for photography into something bigger. My goal is to capture the action, emotion, and unforgettable moments that make sports special.

I'm constantly learning, improving my skills, and working toward becoming a better photographer every day.
